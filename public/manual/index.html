1What is Moneta?

Moneta is a money diary. You write down what you spend and what you earn, and Moneta shows you where your money goes.

  • Add a spend in seconds, for example "Lunch ৳250", with a big number pad.
  • See your totals: how much you have, and what you spent today, this week and this month.
  • Plan with budgets ("max ৳2,500 for fun this month") and savings goals ("new laptop").
  • Never forget bills, loans and money friends owe you.
  • Any currency: Taka, Rupee, Dollar, Euro and many more, even mixed together.
  • You choose where your data lives: in your account (the same data on every phone and computer), or only on this device without any account. You can switch any time.
Good to knowThe pictures in this manual use sample ("demo") data and the Taka (৳) currency. Your screen shows your own numbers.

2Get Moneta

Use it in a web browser, or install the Android app. Both look and work the same.

Option A: Website (any phone or computer)

  1. Open moneta-three-wheat.vercel.app (or moneta-th.vercel.app) in Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox.
  2. That's it. To keep it like an app, use the browser menu → Install app or Add to Home screen.

Option B: Android app (APK)

  1. On your Android phone, open the website and tap Download on the "Get Moneta for Android" banner. You can also go to Settings → Download Android app (APK), or download directly from GitHub Releases:
    github.com/towfiqbinhasan/Moneta/releases/latest
  2. Open the downloaded file Moneta.apk.
  3. If Android says "For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps", tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.
  4. Tap Install, then Open.

Updating the Android app

  • Safe in-place updates: Moneta is signed with an official permanent release key. When you install an updated APK, all your local data, categories, and settings stay completely safe.
  • Automated Cloud Builds: Every release is automatically built and tested through GitHub Actions CI/CD with verified Android SDK & Java environments.
  • Deep Linking: The app includes native deep linking (app.moneta.tracker://auth-callback) so Google and email sign-ins return seamlessly back into the app.
  • When a new version is out, a blue bar appears at the top: New version available. Tap Update, then Install.
  • You can check yourself any time: Settings → scroll to the bottom → Check for updates.
Only download from the links aboveMoneta APKs from other places may be fake.

3First start: set it up in 1 minute

The first time you open Moneta, it asks 4 short questions.

Step 1: Choose your language

Tap English, বাংলা, Deutsch, Norsk or हिन्दी, then tap Continue. You can change it later.

First, the sign-in screen. Sign in (or create a free account) to keep your data in your account. If you already used Moneta on another device, all your data comes back and these setup steps are skipped. Don't want an account? Tap This device only below the sign-in box.

On the website you also see Get Moneta for Android here.

Step 2: Your country

Type your country (for example Bangladesh) and tap it. Moneta then picks your currency and date style for you.

Step 3: How money looks

The big preview shows how amounts will look. Choose how numbers are grouped (12,34,567 is the lakh style used in Bangladesh and India) and the digits (123 or ১২৩). Tap Continue.

Step 4: Make it yours

  1. Type your name. It is used in the greeting.
  2. Pick a style (Receipt, Glass or Brutal). It only changes the look.
  3. Pick a category set: Standard, Student, Family, South Asia or Freelancer.
  4. Load demo data: turn this on only if you want to try Moneta with sample entries. You can remove them later (see Settings).
  5. Tap Start using Moneta.

4The Home screen

Everything important on one screen.

  1. Top: greeting and today's date. The 3 small buttons change the style, switch light/dark, and choose what Home shows.
  2. Total balance: all your money together. Below it is the income and expense for this month, and each account (Cash, Bank…). Swipe the accounts sideways to see more.
  3. Quick buttons: Expense, Income, Transfer, Goals.
  4. Today / This week / This month: how much you spent.
  5. Needs attention: budgets that are almost used up, bills due soon, and money due from people.
  6. Budgets and more cards below. Scroll down.

The bottom bar

Home · Transactions · the big + button (add an entry) · Reports · More

Further down

  • Spending by category (which kinds of things cost you most).
  • Charts of the last days and months.
  • This month vs last month and simple tips.
  • Health score: a 0–100 score for how well this month is going.
  • Goals and your recent entries.
TipDon't need a card? Tap the sliders button at the top of Home (or Settings → Dashboard widgets) to hide it or move it.

5Add money in or out

This is what you will do most. It takes about 5 seconds.

  1. Tap the big blue + at the bottom.
  2. Choose at the top: Expense (money out), Income (money in) or Transfer (moving money between your own accounts, for example Bank → Cash).
  3. Type the amount on the number pad. You can even calculate: 120 + 80.
  4. Tap a category such as Food or Transport.
  5. Tap Save. Done!

Extra options

  • Account: choose Cash, Bank, bKash… (Moneta remembers your last one).
  • Currency: tap ৳ BDT to enter another currency.
  • Date, note, #tags, receipt photo: open the details part below the categories.
  • Repeat (for example every month) and Save as template.
  • Templates (the ☆ chips like "Coffee ৳300") fill everything with one tap.
Made a mistake?After saving, tap Undo on the message at the bottom. To change an entry later, tap it in the list. To delete it, open it and tap the bin, or swipe it to the left.

6See all your entries

Tap Transactions in the bottom bar.

  • Entries are grouped by day, newest first.
  • Search by note, tag or category, or use the filter button (date, account, category, amount…).
  • Tap an entry to see or change it.
  • Swipe left to delete (you can Undo). Swipe right to copy it as a new entry.

7Reports

Tap Reports in the bottom bar to understand your money.

  1. Choose Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Yearly or Custom.
  2. Use ‹ › to move to the previous or next period.
  3. See Income, Expense and Net (what is left).
  4. Insights explain the numbers in plain words, like "You spent 9% less than last month".
  5. Charts and a list by category are below.
  6. Calendar at the top shows each day's spending as colours.

You can also export a report as PDF, Excel or CSV to print or share.

9Accounts

An account is any place you keep money: Cash, a bank, a card, bKash, Nagad, PayPal…

  1. Go to More → Accounts.
  2. Tap + Add, type a name, pick a type, currency, icon and colour.
  3. Opening balance: how much is in it right now. Moneta adds and subtracts from there.
  • Tap an account to change it.
  • Archive an account you don't use any more. Its history stays.
  • The total at the top is all accounts together, in your main currency.

10Categories

Categories are the kinds of spending (Food, Rent…) and income (Salary…).

  • Add: tap + Add, give a name, icon and colour.
  • Sub-category: choose a "parent", for example Food → Restaurant.
  • Rename, recolour or delete: tap a category.
  • Change the order: use the arrows.
  • Load a category set adds a ready-made set (Student, Family…).
Deleting is safeEntries in a deleted category are kept. They become "Uncategorised", or move to the parent category.

11Budgets

A budget is a spending limit, for example "Food: ৳8,000 per month".

  1. Go to More → Budgets → + Add.
  2. Choose how often (daily, weekly, monthly, yearly), the amount, and which categories. Leave categories empty for an overall budget.
  3. Save.
  • Green = fine, yellow = near the limit, red = over.
  • Moneta tells you how much you can still spend per day.
  • Rollover: money you did not use is added to next month.

12Savings goals

Save for something: a laptop, an emergency fund, Eid shopping…

  1. More → Savings goals → + Add. Enter the name, target amount and (optional) the date you want it by. You can add a picture.
  2. When you put money aside, tap + Add / withdraw on the goal.
  • The ring shows how far you are (for example 54%).
  • Moneta tells you how much to save per day to make it in time, and when you will reach it at your current speed.
  • You get a small celebration at 25%, 50%, 75% and 100%. 🎉

13Debts & loans

Keep track of money you lent or borrowed.

  • Two tabs: Owed to me (people owe you) and I owe.
  • + Add: person, amount, due date, note. Turn on Remind me to get a reminder near the due date.
  • When someone pays back (all or part), open it and tap Record payment.
  • When everything is paid, it is hidden from the list. Tap Show settled to see it again.

14Split bills

Paid for dinner with friends? Split it fairly.

  1. More → Split bills → + Add.
  2. Enter a title and the total, and add each person.
  3. Choose Split equally or Custom amounts (type each person's share).
  4. Tick who has already paid.
  5. Keep Add unpaid shares to "Owed to me" on, and everyone who has not paid is added to Debts automatically.

15Bill reminders

Never miss rent, electricity, internet or school fees.

  1. More → Bill reminders → + Add: name, amount, due date and how often it repeats.
  2. Bills due soon appear on Home → Needs attention.
  3. When you pay, tap Paid. Moneta adds the expense for you and moves the due date to the next month.

16Repeating entries & templates

Let Moneta add regular entries for you.

  • Recurring: salary, rent, subscriptions… When adding an entry, choose Repeat (daily, weekly, monthly, yearly). Moneta then adds it by itself on each date, even if you did not open the app that day.
  • Here you can pause (⏸), edit or delete a repeating entry.
  • Templates: saved entries (like "Bus ৳150"). They appear as ☆ chips when you add an entry. One tap fills everything in.

17Trips & events

Going on a trip, or planning a wedding? Keep its spending together.

  1. More → Trips & events → + Add: name, currency (for example Thai Baht), budget and dates.
  2. Tap Start trip mode. While a trip is on, a coloured bar shows at the top, and every new entry belongs to the trip and uses its currency.
  3. See the trip's total, and how it compares to the budget.
  4. Tap Stop trip mode when you are home.

18Settings

Go to More → Settings. Here are the parts most people use.

Language

Change the language any time. Bangla, Hindi, German and Norwegian are included.

Currency & format

Your main currency, the number style (lakh or million), the date format, the first day of the week, and the month start day (useful if your salary comes on the 25th).

Exchange rates

Moneta gets today's rates from the internet to convert other currencies. You can type your own rate too.

  • Appearance: style (Receipt / Glass / Brutal), Light, Dark or Auto, and your favourite colour.
  • Profile & Photos: your name and avatar picture. Pictures (avatars and expense receipt photos) are compressed automatically on your device into compact Base64 format (under 30 KB). They open instantly with zero loading delay and require no expensive external cloud storage!
  • Dashboard widgets: show, hide and reorder the cards on Home.
  • App lock: ask for a PIN (4–6 digits; fingerprint or face also works if your device supports it) every time Moneta opens. Choose a PIN you will remember!
  • Daily reminder: a notification at your chosen time if you have not added anything that day.

19Sign in & use on many devices

With an account, everything you add is saved in your account, so you can sign in on any phone or computer and see the same data. Changing or losing a phone does not lose your data.

Choose: account or this device only

Settings → Cloud sync → Where is your data saved?

  • Cloud (your account): saved in your account and on every device you sign in on.
  • This device only: no account. Data stays on this phone or browser only.
  • Switching to cloud asks you to sign in, then uploads this device's data to your account. Switching to this device only signs you out but keeps all data on this device.
  1. Go to Settings → Cloud sync and tap Sign in.
  2. New here? Tap Create account and enter your name, e-mail and a password. Then open the e-mail Moneta sends you and tap the link to confirm.
  3. Already have an account? Enter your e-mail and password and tap Sign in, or use Google if you see that button.
  4. On your other phone or computer, sign in with the same account. Your data appears there too, and every change shows up on all devices within seconds.
  • Forgot your password? Tap Forgot password? and enter your email. Moneta sends a password reset link pointing directly to your live app (e.g. moneta-three-wheat.vercel.app). Tap it on your phone or computer to set a new password, then log back into the app smoothly.
  • Realtime Multi-Device Sync: Every change you make on your phone (like adding an expense) instantly syncs to your desktop browser and vice versa via Supabase Realtime without manual refreshing.
  • Sync status shows at the top (computer) and in Settings: Synced, Syncing… or Offline. Offline is fine: changes are saved locally and sent automatically when the internet is back.
  • Sign out: tap your picture → Sign out. Moneta first uploads your latest changes, then removes the data from this device only. Sign in again (here or anywhere) to get it all back.

20Where your data is kept (Local-First Privacy)

Moneta uses a modern Local-First architecture. Your device is the primary home for your data, giving you instant speed and complete offline freedom.

WhereWhat is kept there
This phone / browser (IndexedDB)Your local database: 16 structured tables storing all transactions, categories, budgets, and compressed photos right inside your device's memory. Works 100% offline with zero latency.
Your account (Supabase Cloud)Your secure encrypted mirror: Backs up your entries, accounts, budgets, goals, and settings. Protected by Postgres Row-Level Security (RLS) so only your authenticated user ID can access it.
App Lock PIN (Local only)Never uploaded to cloud: Your security PIN and biometric keys stay strictly locked inside your device for maximum privacy.

Keep your money diary safe

  • Tombstone delete protection: When you delete an item, Moneta logs a smart deletion marker so multi-device sync never accidentally revives deleted data.
  • Your account already keeps a copy. A Backup file now and then is still a good extra safety net.
  • Turn on App lock if other people use your phone.
  • Moneta never asks for your bank password or card number. It only stores what you type into it.

22Questions & problems

QuestionAnswer
Do I need internet?No for "This device only". With an account, only to sign in the first time on a device; after that Moneta also works offline and uploads your changes later.
Is it free?Yes.
I added something wrong.Tap the entry in Transactions, fix it and save. Or delete it (swipe left).
My balance looks wrong.Check the opening balance of each account (More → Accounts), and that transfers go between the right accounts.
How do I remove the sample data?Settings → Backup & data → Remove demo data.
I forgot my App lock PIN.With an account: reinstall the app (or clear the site data) and sign in again, everything comes back. Without an account, the data can't be opened without the PIN, so choose one you remember.
Reminders don't show.Allow notifications for Moneta in your phone's settings. In a browser, reminders only come while Moneta is open.
The Android app won't install.Allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser or Files app (see section 2).
Will updating the APK delete my data?No! The APK is signed with a permanent cryptographic key, so updates install directly over your existing app without touching your data.
How does password reset work?Tap Forgot password? on the login screen. It sends a secure email link directing you to your live web app where you can set your new password, then sign right back into the mobile app.
Why do profile pictures and receipts work offline?Photos are compressed into lightweight Base64 strings right on your phone, so they load in zero seconds even with no internet connection.
I have a new phone.With an account: install Moneta and sign in, everything is there. Without an account: make a Backup on the old phone and Restore it on the new one (or switch to Cloud first).
Data is missing on my other device.Make sure both are signed in to the same account and connected to the internet. Then tap Settings → Cloud sync → Sync now.
